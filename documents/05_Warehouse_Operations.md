# L&T SCM

## Warehouse Operations

### Purpose

Warehouse operations ensure safe storage and efficient handling of construction materials.

## Warehouse Process

1. Material Arrival
2. Inspection
3. Barcode Labeling
4. Storage Allocation
5. Inventory Update
6. Material Issue

## Storage Zones

| Zone | Materials |
|--------|------------|
| Zone A | Cement |
| Zone B | Steel |
| Zone C | Electrical |
| Zone D | Plumbing |

## Material Handling Rules

- Use forklifts for heavy loads.
- Stack cement properly.
- Protect steel from corrosion.
- Separate hazardous materials.

## Safety Requirements

Workers must wear:

- Safety Helmet
- Gloves
- Safety Shoes
- Reflective Jacket

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why are materials barcoded?

Barcodes improve inventory tracking and reduce manual errors.

### How often are warehouse audits performed?

Monthly, with additional surprise inspections.
