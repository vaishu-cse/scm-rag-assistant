# L&T SCM

## Inventory Management

### Purpose

Inventory management ensures construction materials are available when needed without excessive stock accumulation.

## Inventory Categories

- Raw Materials
- Consumables
- Spare Parts
- Safety Equipment
- Heavy Machinery Components

## Stock Levels

| Level | Description |
|---------|--------------|
| Minimum Stock | Lowest safe quantity |
| Reorder Level | Quantity that triggers procurement |
| Maximum Stock | Highest allowed inventory |

### Example

| Material | Current Stock | Reorder Level |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| Cement | 220 Bags | 300 Bags |
| Steel Rod | 18 Tons | 25 Tons |
| Paint | 120 Liters | 80 Liters |

## Inventory Movement

### Goods Receipt

Materials are inspected before entering inventory.

### Material Issue

Materials issued to construction sites are recorded against project codes.

## Cycle Count

Inventory verification is performed:

- Daily for high-value items
- Weekly for fast-moving items
- Monthly for regular stock

## Common Questions

### When should procurement be initiated?

Procurement begins when stock reaches the reorder level.

### Why is cycle counting important?

It prevents inventory mismatches and improves stock accuracy.
