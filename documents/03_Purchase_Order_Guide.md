# L&T SCM

## Purchase Order Guide

### Purpose

A Purchase Order (PO) is an official document issued to vendors authorizing material procurement.

## Purchase Order Lifecycle

1. PR Approved
2. Vendor Finalized
3. PO Generated
4. Vendor Acknowledgement
5. Shipment
6. Goods Receipt
7. Invoice Matching
8. Payment

## Sample Purchase Order

| Field | Value |
|---------|---------|

| Quantity | 50 Tons |

## Three-Way Matching

Before payment, three documents must match:

- Purchase Order
- Goods Receipt Note
- Vendor Invoice

## PO Status

| Status | Meaning |
|----------|-----------|
| Draft | Not issued |
| Approved | Ready for vendor |
| Released | Sent to vendor |
| Partially Delivered | Some items received |
| Closed | Fully completed |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can a PO be modified?

Yes, through a controlled PO amendment after approval.

### When is a PO closed?

After full delivery and payment completion.
