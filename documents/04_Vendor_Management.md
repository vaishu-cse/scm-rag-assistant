# L&T SCM

## Vendor Management

### Purpose

Vendor management ensures reliable supplier relationships throughout construction projects.

## Vendor Categories

- Civil Materials
- Electrical Equipment
- Mechanical Equipment
- Transport Services
- Safety Equipment

## Vendor Registration

Required documents include:

- GST Registration
- PAN
- Bank Details
- Quality Certifications
- Previous Project References

## Vendor Evaluation Criteria

| Criteria | Weight |
|------------|----------|
| Quality | 35% |
| Cost | 25% |
| Delivery | 20% |
| Compliance | 10% |
| Support | 10% |

## Vendor Performance Scorecard

| Metric | Target |
|----------|-----------|
| On-Time Delivery | 95% |
| Quality Acceptance | 98% |
| Invoice Accuracy | 100% |

## Vendor Risk Indicators

High-risk vendors may have:

- Late deliveries
- Quality failures
- Contract violations
- Missing certifications

## Common Questions

### What makes a preferred vendor?

Consistent quality, timely delivery, and compliance.

### Can a vendor be suspended?

Yes, for repeated performance or compliance failures.
