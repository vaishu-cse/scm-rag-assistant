# L&T SCM

## Logistics and Delivery

### Purpose

Logistics coordinates transportation of materials from suppliers to project sites.

## Delivery Process

1. Dispatch from Vendor
2. Transport Tracking
3. Site Arrival
4. Inspection
5. Goods Receipt

## Shipment Status

| Status | Meaning |
|----------|------------|
| Scheduled | Delivery planned |
| In Transit | Vehicle moving |
| Delayed | Arrival postponed |
| Delivered | Received at site |

## Delivery Priorities

| Priority | Example |
|-----------|-----------|
| Critical | Structural Steel |
| High | Cement |
| Normal | Paint |
| Low | Office Supplies |

## Common Delay Reasons

- Traffic restrictions
- Weather conditions
- Vehicle breakdown
- Vendor dispatch delay

## Delivery Checklist

Before accepting delivery:

- Verify PO Number
- Check quantity
- Inspect quality
- Record damages
- Update inventory

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What should be done if materials arrive damaged?

Record the damage, notify procurement, and raise a Goods Receipt discrepancy.

### How is shipment tracking performed?

Using transport updates, delivery acknowledgements, and warehouse receipt confirmation.
